Sharing & sending

How do I send a meme to WhatsApp?

Open any meme, tap the share button, and choose WhatsApp from the share sheet. Whamr hands the file straight to WhatsApp so you can pick a chat and send — no download step needed on most devices.

Which platforms can I share to?

Nine: WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Instagram, Discord, Signal, your device's native share menu, copy link, and direct download.

Do I need an account to send?

No. Browsing, searching and sending are all free with no sign-in. You only need an account if you want favourites to sync across devices or to use creator tools.

Sticker packs

How do I build a sticker pack?

Go to the pack builder, add up to 30 stickers, give the pack a name and publisher, then export. Whamr generates a ready-to-install WhatsApp pack (a .wastickers file) with the correct sizes and tray icon.

Why are some memes skipped from packs?

WhatsApp sticker packs support still images (converted to 512×512 WebP). Video memes are skipped for now — animated-sticker export is on the roadmap.

Search & finding

How does the AI search work?

Type in plain language — even Naija slang like "sapa", "wahala" or "japa". Whamr expands synonyms and uses fuzzy matching, so typos and near-misses still surface the right memes by title, category and tags.

Can I browse by category?

Yes. Use the category pills on the library page to filter across all 11 categories, from Naija and Reactions to Dance, Sports and Birthday.

Account & favourites

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen choose "Forgot password", enter your email, and we'll send a reset link. Open it and set a new password. The link is single-use and expires after 60 minutes for security.

Do my favourites sync across devices?

Yes — when you're signed in, hearting a meme saves it to your account and it appears on any device you log into. Signed-out favourites are kept locally and merged in when you sign in.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. You can use Google sign-in or an email and password — both give you synced favourites and access to the same account features.

Creator tools

What do creator tools include?

On the Creator plan you get analytics on your uploads, eligibility for the verified badge, a tip jar with an earnings wallet, and priority placement in search and trending. See the pricing page for details.

How do tips and earnings work?

Supporters can tip your memes. Tips go to you on a 90/10 split (you keep 90%), collect in your wallet, and you withdraw to your bank when you're ready.

Community

How do I report a comment or meme?

Use the report option on any comment or piece of content. Reports go to our moderation queue and are reviewed quickly. You can report anonymously.

Who can delete comments?

You can always delete your own comments. Moderators can remove comments that break the community guidelines.
