Changelog

What's new.

Every improvement we ship, version by version. The whole history of Whamr getting faster and better at the send.

v2.1 Feature June 2026
  • Send stickers straight to WhatsApp. A new direct sticker-send pipeline (currently in Meta's review queue for native rendering).
  • Cloud-synced favourites. Heart a meme on one device, find it on the next — synced when you're signed in.
  • Public discussion threads on every meme, with reporting and moderation.
v2.0 Major May 2026
  • Whamr v2 redesign. New dark interface, faster grid, and the sunset brand system.
  • Sticker pack builder. Bundle up to 30 stickers and export a ready-to-install WhatsApp pack.
  • 9-platform share sheet — WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Instagram, Discord, Signal, native share, copy link, and download.
v1.5 Feature April 2026
  • AI natural-language search. Search in plain words — Naija slang like "sapa", "wahala" and "japa" maps to the right memes.
  • Synonym expansion & fuzzy matching so typos and near-misses still find what you mean.
v1.2 Improvement March 2026
  • Cloudflare R2 hosting. Moved all media to R2 for faster, cheaper global delivery.
  • Accounts & Google sign-in with secure password reset.
v1.0 Launch February 2026
  • Whamr launches. A real, properly-tagged library of Naija and global memes, built for one thing: the send.
  • 11 categories, thousands of video clips and WebP stickers, no account required to browse.
