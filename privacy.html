Privacy Policy

We keep this simple. Here is exactly what data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Minimal data No ads No data selling You control your data

Last updated: 21 May 2026

Note

This is a working draft written for clarity. Before public launch, and especially before accounts or payments go live, Whamr should have this policy reviewed by a data protection professional to make sure it meets the rules that apply in the regions Whamr serves.

1 Who We Are

Whamr operates this meme and sticker platform. We are responsible for the personal data processed through the Service. For privacy questions, reach us through the contact details on the site.

2 What We Collect Today

Right now, Whamr works without requiring you to create an account, so the data we handle is minimal:

2.1 On-device data

Your saved favourites are stored in your own browser on your device. They are not sent to us or linked to your identity.

2.2 Usage data

Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers may log basic technical information such as pages visited, approximate timing, browser type, and IP address. This is used to keep the Service running and secure, not to build a profile of you.

2.3 Sharing actions

When you share a meme, the share happens directly between your device and the messaging app you choose (WhatsApp, Telegram, and so on). We do not see who you send it to.

3 What We Do Not Do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not require your real name to use the Service.

4 Third-Party Services

Whamr relies on a few trusted providers to operate:

  • Vercel hosts and serves the website.
  • Cloudflare stores and delivers the meme and sticker media.
  • OpenMoji provides some of the sticker artwork under an open licence.

These providers process technical data on our behalf to deliver the Service. They have their own privacy practices.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

Whamr uses your browser's local storage to remember things like your saved favourites. This stays on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6 When Accounts Arrive

Whamr plans to add user accounts and creator features in future. When that happens, we will collect additional data such as an email address and the content you choose to upload, and this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is collected and why before those features go live.

7 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including the right to access it, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Since Whamr currently holds very little personal data, most of what relates to you (your saved favourites) lives on your own device and you can clear it any time through your browser.

To make a request about any data we do hold, contact us through the site.

8 Children's Privacy

Whamr is not directed at young children. When accounts launch, there will be a minimum age to register, and we will not knowingly collect personal data from anyone below that age.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Whamr grows. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, highlight them on the site.

10 Contact

Questions about your privacy on Whamr? Reach us through the contact details on the site and we will respond.
