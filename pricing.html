Pricing questions

Is Whamr really free?

Yes. Browsing the full library, searching, sharing to any platform, saving favourites and building sticker packs are all free, forever, with no card required. Paid plans only add creator and developer tooling on top.

What do I get with Creator?

Creator unlocks analytics on your uploads (sends, reach, your best-performing memes), eligibility for the verified badge, a tip jar with an earnings wallet, and priority placement in search and trending.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month (or annual if you choose). Cancel whenever you like and you keep access until the end of your billing period. No lock-in.

How does annual billing save me money?

Switching to annual takes 20% off the monthly rate — Creator works out to about $5.60/mo and API to about $23.20/mo, billed once a year.

What's included in the API plan?

The API plan gives you programmatic search and send endpoints, the WhatsApp sticker-send integration, 100k requests a month, webhooks, bulk export, and priority support. Get in touch and we'll set you up with keys.

Do creators keep their earnings?

Yes. Tips go to creators on a 90/10 split (you keep 90%). Earnings collect in your wallet and you withdraw to your bank when you're ready.
