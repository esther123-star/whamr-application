Built for the send.

It started with a WhatsApp argument

Someone sent the wrong meme at the wrong moment. The vibe was off. The group chat went quiet. Not because the meme was offensive, but because it was the wrong one entirely. You know the difference. So did we.

We built Whamr because meme culture is real culture, and Naija meme culture especially deserves better tooling. The big global libraries do not know who Patience Ozokwo is. They do not have the 5000 Naira Pawpaw clip. They do not understand the difference between "funny naija" and just "funny." We do.

A real library, properly built

The library has thousands of real files: video clips, WhatsApp stickers, reactions, birthday memes, Nollywood moments, dance clips, and sports reactions. Every one of them categorised, tagged, and ready to send to exactly the right person at exactly the right moment.

No accounts needed to browse. No ads cluttering the page. Just you, the meme, and one tap to send it wherever your people are.

What we believe

Meme culture is real culture

How people communicate through memes is a genuine language. It deserves tools made with care, not an afterthought.

Naija-rooted, globally fluent

Nigerian content is some of the most creative on the internet. We start there, and we build outward.

Built for the way we share

People share in WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, and Twitter threads, not in stock photo libraries. We build for that.

Fast beats fancy

The best meme tool is the one that gets out of your way. Find it, send it, done.
