The story

It started with a WhatsApp argument.

Someone sent the wrong meme at the wrong moment. The vibe was off. The group chat went quiet. Not because the meme was offensive, because it was the Shrek meme when it needed to be the Mr Ibu meme. You know the difference. So did we.

We built Whamr because meme culture is real culture, and Naija meme culture especially deserves better tooling. Giphy doesn't know who Patience Ozokwo is. Tenor doesn't have the 5000 Naira Pawpaw clip. Google doesn't understand the difference between "funny naija" and "funny", but our AI does.

The library now has 9,128 real files: video clips, WhatsApp stickers, reactions, birthday memes, Nollywood moments, dance clips, sports reactions. Every one of them categorised, tagged, and ready to send to exactly the right person at exactly the right moment.

What we believe

Meme culture is real culture.

🌍
Global, not generic

Whamr is built for the way people actually share: in WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, and Twitter threads. Not for stock photo libraries.

🇳🇬
Naija-rooted, globally fluent

Nigerian content is some of the most creative, expressive, and shareable content on the internet. We built for Naija first because that's where the energy is.

🤖
AI that gets the vibe

You shouldn't have to know the exact name of a clip to find it. Type "something funny when your friend is wrong" and the AI figures it out.

🔒
Your content, your rules

You own what you upload. We take 10% on tips. We don't sell your data. We don't run ads. We make money when creators make money.

⚡
Fast or it doesn't work

If it takes more than 3 taps to send a meme, nobody uses it. Every feature is designed around the send.

🤝
Community over algorithm

Dojos, battles, challenges, requests. The community decides what's good, not a black-box recommendation engine.
